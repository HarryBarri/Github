Questions, answered

Do I need technical staff to use Fluxon?

No. We design, build, and maintain the automations. You just point us at the busywork and approve the results.

Is my data secure?

Yes. We follow least-privilege access, encrypt data in transit, and only connect to the systems you explicitly authorize. We never train public models on your data.

What does it cost?

Pricing is based on scope and the hours we save you. Waitlist members get founding-customer pricing — we'll walk you through it on the intro call.

What if an automation makes a mistake?

Critical steps keep a human in the loop. You stay in control with approvals, audit logs, and the ability to pause any automation instantly.
